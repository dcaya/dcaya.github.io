What we bake

Everything is baked from scratch each morning in a dedicated production kitchen you can see from the retail floor. Our menu is deliberately focused: about 45 core items, plus rotating seasonal specials.

Viennoiserie

Laminated butter croissants, almond croissants, pain au chocolat, kouign-amann, pain aux raisins, ham-and-Gruyère croissants and seasonal fruit danishes, made with 82% European-style butter and 72-hour cold-fermented dough.

Artisan breads

Baguette tradition, country sourdough, olive and rosemary fougasse, seeded multigrain, brioche and weekend specialty loaves, baked in a deck oven on a morning and midday schedule.

Pâtisserie

Lemon and seasonal fruit tarts, éclairs, religieuse, Paris-Brest, mille-feuille, opéra, canelés and macarons in 12 rotating flavors.

Custom & wedding cakes

Consultation-led design, tastings and delivery for weddings, birthdays and corporate events. Celebration cakes in 6-inch, 8-inch and 10-inch sizes, tiered wedding cakes, and dessert-table packages.

Café

Quiche Lorraine and vegetarian quiche, tartines, croque monsieur and madame, soup of the day, house granola and yogurt, plus espresso, drip coffee, tea and hot chocolate. Enjoy it in our 28-seat dining room or on the Pacific Avenue sidewalk patio.

Wholesale & holiday pre-orders

Croissants, baguettes and brioche buns for local coffee shops, delivered by 6:30 a.m. Tuesday through Saturday. Seasonal pre-orders include Thanksgiving pies, Christmas bûches de Noël, Galette des Rois, and Valentine's and Mother's Day gift boxes.

About Crumb & Croissant

To bring the craft of a Parisian boulangerie to Santa Cruz: honest ingredients, patient technique, and baked-this-morning goods that make everyday moments and life's milestones feel special.

Crumb & Croissant is a founder-led French bakery and pâtisserie opening on Pacific Avenue in downtown Santa Cruz, California. It brings a true viennoiserie, a pâtisserie case, a cake studio and a café together under one roof, which is rare in Santa Cruz. Our open kitchen lets you watch the head baker shape croissants as you wait.

We serve Santa Cruz residents and downtown workers, the UC Santa Cruz community, visitors on and near Pacific Avenue, and couples, event planners and local businesses who rely on us for cakes, catering and supply.

What guides us

Craft over shortcuts
No par-baked, frozen or commissary-supplied product. Laminated dough rests 72 hours and sourdough ferments overnight.
Local sourcing
Flour from California mills, produce from Monterey Bay farms, and dairy from Northern California creameries wherever quality and supply allow.
Community first
Consistent hiring from the UC Santa Cruz and Cabrillo College culinary and hospitality pipelines, and a daily donation of unsold goods to a local food bank.
Sustainability
Compostable packaging, organics recycling, and a reusable-cup discount.
